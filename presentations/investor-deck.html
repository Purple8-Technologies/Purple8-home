Technology · Why We're Defensible

Three inventions that don't exist
anywhere else.

🧠

Bounded-RSS Storage Engine

Peak memory does not grow with corpus size. The same machine handles 2M or 20M nodes identically — not because of compression or sampling, but because of a seal-and-evict architecture that returns memory to the OS after each write batch. Validated at 8.84M MS MARCO nodes on a 24 GB machine.

No analogue in RocksDB, LMDB, DuckDB, or Redis
⚡

Graph-Guided Vector Search

Instead of fanning out to all N vector segments per query, Purple8 uses BFS over graph edges to pre-select a bounded candidate set, then passes only those segments to the HNSW index. In clustered corpora, >80% of segments are skipped entirely.

No analogue in Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, or pgvector
🤖

MCP-Native from Day One

74 tools across 11 namespaces — AI agents can build, query, and operate the entire backend through natural language with zero human intermediaries. No other infrastructure company ships MCP as a first-class interface, not a bolt-on.

ESG & Competitive Moat

Consolidation is the sustainability strategy.

The current multi-service AI stack isn't just expensive — it's unsustainable at global scale. 8–29 services running 24/7, each with 3× redundancy, for every AI application is a civilisational energy problem.

The Bounded-RSS property = a sustainability property

On a constrained machine, Purple8 auto-configures to fit available RAM. The same corpus runs on 16 GB or 128 GB hardware — you trade time for memory, never correctness or capacity. No vertical scaling required as knowledge bases grow.

This makes AI commercially viable for organisations that cannot afford to re-provision infrastructure every time their knowledge base doubles.
