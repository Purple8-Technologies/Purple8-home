Workflow Automation · A Real Example

From document received
to decision made — fully automated.

Example: Loan Application
Stage 1
Application & documents received
Purple8 DocIntel reads the application — payslips, bank statements, ID documents — and extracts all key data automatically. No manual data entry.
Stage 2
AI risk & policy check
The AI queries your policy knowledge graph and scores the application against lending criteria. Risk flags are surfaced instantly — no analyst needed for routine cases.
Stage 3
Human approval (where required)
Complex cases are escalated to a credit officer with full context. SLA countdown begins. Automatic escalation if the deadline is missed.
Stage 4
Decision recorded & auditable
Every step — document received, AI assessment, human decision — is stored in an immutable audit trail. Regulator-ready from day one.
