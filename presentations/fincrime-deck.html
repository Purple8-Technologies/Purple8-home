Module 2 · Detection

Six crypto crime typologies detected natively

Purple8 detects patterns that single-chain, cloud-based tools miss, including cross-chain layering and DeFi-based laundering.

01
Peeling Chains

Linear 1-in-1-out wallet chains with no economic rationale. Graph detects long-hop paths with no branching, a classic obfuscation technique.

02
Mixing / Tumbling

CoinJoin many-to-many patterns. Timing correlation across inputs flags coordinated tumblers even when individual transactions look clean.

03
Cross-Chain Layering

CEX → DEX → bridge → new chain → CEX. Multi-hop traversal across Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Tron, and Polygon in a single graph query.

04
Both-Sides Betting

Same entity bets YES and NO through different wallets, converting dirty crypto to winnings. Common-input clustering catches it.

05
NFT Wash Trading

Community detection finds wallets buying and selling the same NFT to each other, identifying price inflation rings and mixer-disguised royalties.

06
Structuring

Sub-threshold transactions across multiple wallets. Temporal aggregation on SENT edges with amount and time windows catches coordinated splitting.
