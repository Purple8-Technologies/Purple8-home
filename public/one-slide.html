The Infrastructure Problem

AI is inevitable. The infrastructure behind it is unsustainable.

The Problem

Every AI application runs on 29 services bolted together. That architecture was right for the cloud era. It is wrong for the AI era. Larger models won't fix it. Larger data centres won't fix it. You cannot change physics: distributed systems mean network hops, idle compute, version conflicts, and cost that scales with every service. Each service is fine. The arrangement is the problem. SaaS at this complexity costs millions to build, deploy, run, and maintain. That is not a business model problem. It is a planetary resource problem.

Purple8

One process that replaces the 29. Graph. Vector. Documents. RAG. Workflows. Security. AI agent interface. Born as one system, purpose-built for how AI needs to access, reason over, and act on data. Neurosymbolic by design: industry-agnostic graph algorithms give AI deterministic answers without burning LLM tokens. The graph reasons; the LLM only handles what it must. Private and self-hosted by default: a single ~300 MiB process that runs on-premise, air-gapped, on edge devices, or in any cloud. No elaborate DevOps. No data leaves your environment. Memory bounded by hardware, not data. Zero network hops. The infrastructure correction that makes AI economically and environmentally viable, so it can solve complex problems, not consume complex infrastructure.
